Mono Example

GetMonoPolarizedImages.cpp

This example shows how to obtain the most relevant images from an initial mono RAW file: Degree of Linear Polarization (DoLP), Angle of Linear Polarization (AoLP) and mono images for each polarization angle, among others. The example starts storing the RAW data in a buffer, and then uses multiple functions to compute all the available results.

The available outputs are:

  • A 2x2 grid image with the four polarization angles

  • A single mono image for a specific polarization angle

  • Degree of Linear Polarization (DoLP) image

  • Angle of Linear Polarization (AoLP) image, also available in HSV format

  • A combination of DoLP and AoLP in HSV format, where DoLP is the saturation, and AoLP the hue

To get these images, only the RAW image from the camera is needed.

Images are saved in BMP or RAW format.
